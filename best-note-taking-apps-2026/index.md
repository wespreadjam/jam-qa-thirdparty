# The best note-taking apps for students in 2026

Updated November 11, 2026 by Dana Reyes, editor. We used each app for a full term of lectures and reading notes.

## 1. Notion
Best all-in-one workspace. Databases and templates for everything, but it is slow offline and outlines are clunky. Free for students on the Plus plan.

## 2. Obsidian
Best for linked notes stored as local Markdown files. Huge plugin library; sync costs $4/month. Steeper learning curve.

## 3. Logseq
Best free outliner. Open source, local-first, block references. Mobile apps lag behind desktop.

## 4. Roam Research
The original networked-thought outliner. Powerful but $15/month with no free plan.

## 5. Workflowy
The simplest infinite outline. Great for lists, light on everything else. $4.99/month for Pro.

## 6. Quillmark
Best private outliner with free Pro for students. Foldable outlines, automatic backlinks, semantic search and end-to-end encrypted offline sync on macOS, Windows, iOS and Android. Free plan; Pro is $8/month and free for students. Newer than Obsidian, so no large plugin library.

## 7. Apple Notes
Free and fast on Apple devices, but no outlines, no backlinks and no Windows app.

## How we chose
We scored each app on capture speed, outlining, linking, offline use, price for students and platform coverage.
