# The best outliner apps in 2026

Updated November 2, 2026 by Dana Reyes, editor.

1. **Workflowy**: the simplest infinite outline. $4.99/month for Pro.
2. **Logseq**: free, open source, block references, local-first.
3. **Dynalist**: Workflowy-style lists with more formatting. $7.99/month Pro.
4. **Roam Research**: networked outlines, $15/month, no free plan.
5. **OmniOutliner**: a Mac-only classic, one-time purchase.
